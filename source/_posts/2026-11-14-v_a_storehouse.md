Never thought a storehouse could be so beautiful and thought provoking. An invitation to creative exploration.

[V&A Storehouse](https://www.vam.ac.uk/east/storehouse/visit) presents no apparent logic, segmentation or explanation to many items. I felt on a quest to know how they connect together and the story behind them. I asked myself: _"If I was a curator, how would I craft a narrative in the form of an exhibit?"_

We don't usually give proper credit to good museum curation. Exhibits are just there, available, neat, sometimes free of charge, and many of us just *run* through them.

If a museum would start with a storehouse section before any curated exhibitions, almost like an inverted IKEA layout, then I think many more would truly value what they are experiencing.

_You don't know what you've got until it's gone._
